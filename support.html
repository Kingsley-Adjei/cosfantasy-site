Support

Common problems and how to fix them. If yours isn't here, write to us.

Common questions

Google sign-in opens a browser and comes back to nothing

Usually a stale browser session. Close COS Fantasy completely, reopen it, and try again. If it keeps happening, clear your browser's cookies for accounts.google.com and retry.

Why is Google the only way to sign in?

Email sign-in needs a verified sending domain so verification codes actually reach you. That's coming. Google sign-in works now, and your team carries over when email arrives — nothing is lost.

My points haven't updated

Points are entered after each match finishes, not live during play. Pull down on the My Team screen to refresh. If a match ended more than a day ago and your points still look wrong, email us with the gameweek.

A league code isn't working

Codes are case-insensitive but exact — check for a confused 0 and O, or 1 and I. You also need a team before joining a league. If it still fails, ask the league creator to re-share the code from the Leagues screen.

I'm not getting notifications

Check Profile for the alert types you've enabled, then check COS Fantasy has notification permission in your device settings. Notifications don't work on emulators — only on a real phone.

The app says it can't connect

The server sleeps when idle and takes up to a minute to wake. Wait, then pull to refresh. If it persists across several minutes and your connection is fine, tell us.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete Account, or see the deletion page if you've already uninstalled.

Still stuck

Tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. If you saw an error with a reference code, include it — it points us straight at the problem.
