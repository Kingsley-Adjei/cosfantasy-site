Delete Your
Account

Two ways to do it. Both remove everything, and neither can be undone.

Fastest — if you still have the app

Delete it in the app

  1. Open COS Fantasy and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when asked.

Deletion happens immediately. You will be signed out and returned to the sign-in screen.

If you have uninstalled or cannot sign in

Ask us by email

Email us from the address on your account. We need it to come from that address so we can confirm the account is yours.

Email a deletion request

Or write to support@cosfantasy.app with the subject Delete my account. We confirm within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

Deleted Your account and email address
Deleted Your team name, squad, and captain selection
Deleted Your transfer history and points total
Deleted Every league membership, and any private league you created once its last member leaves
Deleted Your notification token and usage records
Kept Match results and scoring records, with every reference to you removed. Nothing kept identifies you.

This cannot be undone. There is no recovery window and no archived copy. If you sign up again later, you start from zero — new team, no points, no league history.

Signed in with Google?

Deleting your COS Fantasy account does not touch your Google account. It only removes the connection between them and everything COS Fantasy stored. You can also revoke our access from your Google account permissions page, though deleting here already does that.
