The rules for using COS Fantasy. Short, because the app is free and there isn't much to argue about.

Last updated 24 July 2026  ·  Version 1.0

01

Agreeing to these terms

By creating an account or using COS Fantasy, you agree to what's on this page. If you don't, don't use the app — and if you already have an account, you can delete it at any time from Profile.

COS Fantasy is operated by an independent student developer at Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology, Kumasi, Ghana. These terms are governed by Ghanaian law.

02

Your account

You sign in with Google. You're responsible for keeping that Google account secure — anyone who can access it can access your COS Fantasy team.

  • One account per person. Multiple accounts to gain league advantage will be removed.
  • You must be 13 or older.
  • Don't share your account or let someone else manage your team in a league you're competing in.
03

Playing fairly

This is a game between students. The rules exist so it stays worth playing.

  • Don't use bots, scripts, or automated tools to manage your team.
  • Don't attempt to access other users' accounts or data.
  • Don't probe, scan, or attack the app or its servers.
  • Don't use offensive, misleading, or impersonating team and league names.
  • Don't attempt to manipulate scoring or exploit bugs. If you find one, tell us.

If you find a security flaw, email support@cosfantasy.app instead of using it. We'd rather fix it than compete with you over it.

04

Scoring and results

Points are entered manually after each match by an administrator. We do our best to get them right, but mistakes happen — a mis-typed score, a missed goal, a player recorded against the wrong team.

If you think your points are wrong, email us with the gameweek and what you expected. We'll correct genuine errors. Corrections may change standings retrospectively.

Scoring rules, budgets, and transfer allowances may change between seasons. We'll tell you in the app before a change takes effect.

05

No money, no prizes

COS Fantasy is free. There are no entry fees, no purchases, no subscriptions, and no cash prizes. It is not gambling, and it is not a real-money game.

If any league you're in involves money changing hands between players, that's between you and them — it has nothing to do with COS Fantasy and we take no part in it.

06

Availability

The app runs on free hosting tiers. That means it will sometimes be slow to start, occasionally unavailable, and may go down without warning. We don't guarantee uptime.

We may change features, pause the service, or shut it down entirely. If we shut down permanently, we'll give at least 30 days' notice in the app where we reasonably can.

07

Your content

Team names, league names, and anything else you type stay yours. By entering them you allow us to display them to other players in your leagues — which is the whole point of a league table.

We may remove or rename anything offensive, impersonating, or otherwise inappropriate, and may suspend accounts that repeatedly do it.

08

Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time from Profile, or by emailing us — see the deletion page. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

We may suspend or remove accounts that breach these terms. Where it's reasonable to do so, we'll explain why first.

To the extent Ghanaian law allows, we're not liable for lost data, lost points, missed deadlines caused by the app being unavailable, or any indirect loss arising from using it. Nothing here limits liability that cannot legally be limited.
