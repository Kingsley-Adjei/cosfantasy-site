Privacy
Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how to get rid of it. Written to be read, not skimmed past.

Last updated 24 July 2026  ·  Version 1.0

01

Who runs this app

COS Fantasy is a fantasy football app for students, built and operated by an independent student developer at Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology in Kumasi, Ghana.

COS Fantasy is an independent student project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by KNUST, its College of Science, or any professional football league or club.

Questions about anything on this page go to support@cosfantasy.app.

02

What we collect

Everything below is collected because a specific feature needs it. There is no advertising in COS Fantasy, and nothing here is sold.

Email address
Account
Comes from Google when you sign in. It identifies your account and appears to other managers in leagues you join.
Google account ID
Sign-in
A reference number from Google that lets us recognise you on your next sign-in. We never receive your Google password.
Team & league activity
Gameplay
Your team name, squad selections, captain choice, transfers, points, and league memberships. This is the app.
Notification token
Alerts
A device identifier issued by Expo, used to send match and deadline alerts. Only stored if you allow notifications.
Screen usage
Diagnostics
Which screens you open and for roughly how long, so we can find the slow and broken parts. Not linked to your identity for analysis.

What we never collect

  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Payment details — COS Fantasy is free and has no purchases
  • Anything from other apps on your device
03

Who else sees it

A few services process data on our behalf. Each one gets only what it needs to do its job.

  • Google — verifies who you are when you sign in.
  • Supabase — handles the sign-in exchange between Google and our servers.
  • CockroachDB — stores your account and team data.
  • Render — runs the servers the app talks to.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications to your device.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to build advertising profiles. We would disclose information if required by Ghanaian law or a valid legal order, and nowhere else.

Visible to other players

Inside a league, other managers can see your team name, your points, your rank, and your squad. Your email is shown alongside your team in league standings. Nothing else about you is visible to them.

04

How long we keep it

Your account data stays for as long as your account exists. Delete your account and it is removed immediately — see section 06.

Usage diagnostics are capped at the most recent 500 events per device and overwrite themselves as you keep using the app. Server logs are kept for 30 days for debugging and security, then discarded.

05

How it is protected

  • Everything between the app and our servers travels over HTTPS.
  • The database connection is encrypted and certificate-verified.
  • Your sign-in session is held in your device's secure keystore, not in ordinary app storage.
  • We never see or store your Google password.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise. If we discover a breach affecting your data, we will tell affected users by email and post a notice on this page.

06

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and everything attached to it at any time. There are two ways.

In the app

Open Profile, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete Account. Confirm, and the deletion happens immediately.

By email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email support@cosfantasy.app from the address on your account with the subject Delete my account. We will confirm within 30 days.

Deleted permanently: your account, email address, team, squad, transfer history, points, league memberships, notification token, and usage records. Private leagues you created are removed once the last member leaves.

Retained: nothing that identifies you. Aggregate match and scoring records stay, but your name and email are gone from them. This cannot be undone — there is no recovery window.

07

Your other choices

  • Notifications — turn any category off in Profile, or all of them in your device settings.
  • See your data — your profile screen shows everything we hold. Email us for a copy in a portable format.
  • Fix something wrong — email us and we will correct it.

Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) gives you rights over your personal data, including access and correction. Exercising any of them costs nothing.

08

Children

COS Fantasy is intended for university students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

09

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. For anything that meaningfully affects how your data is handled, we will notify you in the app before it takes effect.
